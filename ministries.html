Ministries

Find your place to grow.

We all need a place to grow — a consistent weekly experience with people who care. We learn a common language, share a common bond with the Father, and share our gifts and passions to make our church home a place you want to come to.

SOZO Kids

A safe, vibrant place for young hearts.

No church has a future if it does not focus on making an engaging and safe place for young people. We know that children sense and understand spiritual things even as babies — so we care for them, comfort them, and give parents a place where they can confidently focus on their own time with God. Our goal is not to entertain the children but to engage them in a very real understanding of the gospel.

Nursery (birth – age 2)

A hub of unconditional love, play time, and crafts. The focus is simply to give your little ones the opportunity to just play and be themselves while they’re cared for by our incredible SOZO Kids leaders.

Toddler & Preschool (ages 2 – 5)

Led by team leaders with years of experience. Your kids can expect upbeat praise songs, delicious snacks, and an unlimited amount of toys — all introducing Jesus in a loving, inclusive way.

Jungle House (1st – 6th grade)

A dynamic, Bible-centered ministry full of singing, music, puppets, and scripture. They dance, they pray, they read, and they interact in an environment full of wonder!

Check-In

SOZO Kids check-in begins at 10:15 AM every Sunday morning — just head to the SOZO Kids check-in room. We’re also looking for more SOZO Kids Servant Leaders — reach out if you feel called to be part of it.

SOZO Groups

Life is better, together.

SOZO Groups bring people together to embrace one another across the generations — Men’s and Women’s groups meeting through special launch events and regular gatherings. It’s a unique opportunity to come together across generations and build closer connections.

We’re also home to SOZO Youth — summer hangs, movie nights, and fall events where students belong and grow.
