About Us

Saved. Healed. Delivered.
That's the whole point.

We're SOZO Loveland — a Spirit-led, Bible-based church in Loveland, Ohio. Here's who we are and what we're about.

Why SOZO?

SOZO is the Greek word the New Testament uses for the full work of Jesus — not just forgiveness, but wholeness. Every time you read "saved" in Scripture, there's a good chance the word underneath is sozo.

SAVED

Rescued

Brought out of darkness and into life. Grace that meets you exactly where you are.

HEALED

Restored

Made whole — body, mind, and spirit. God still heals, still restores.

DELIVERED

Set Free

Released from what held you. Freedom you can walk in every day.

Our Vision

The Journey in Three Steps

We open our hearts to embrace one another and link together for the kingdom.

We know we're all on a faith journey. Being a Christian isn't easy, and it isn't finished in a day. When we accept Jesus, the journey begins — and none of us walk it perfectly, but in Christ we are being perfected every day. Our vision gives us signposts: Open, Embrace, and Link.

1

OPEN

Open your heart — to God, to His Word, and to the people He's placed around you. Openness is where transformation starts.

2

EMBRACE

Embrace one another — welcomed as you are, the way Jesus would, and walked with toward who you were made to be.

3

LINK

Link together for the kingdom — joined arm in arm, generation to generation, building something bigger than any one of us.

What We Believe

Our Values

Not slogans on a wall — convictions that shape how we worship, serve, and love.

Spirit Led

The Holy Spirit is in the earth today to lead us into the wisdom of God and guide every step of our lives.

Bible Based

The Bible is the inerrant Word of God — the foundation of what we believe and what we teach.

Family Friendly

The family is the foundation not only of the kingdom, but of Christians growing to maturity.

Preference Free

God is not a respecter of persons. He doesn't favor one group over another — and neither do we.

Independent

Christ is the head of the Church, and each church is given a unique role and purpose in His overall plan.

Team Based, Body Oriented

Each member is a vital part of the local church's ability to achieve its mission.

Generational

Each generation has something to give to the next — and something to receive from the last.

Authentic Faith · Luke 2:52

Our walk with God is both our experience and our reality.

Open Hearts & Minds · 1 Cor 6:11

We relate to others like Jesus would — welcoming them as they are.

Relationships First · 2 Cor 5:16

Our goal is to know and to be known — growing with God and people.

Responsible & Accountable · Eph 4:14–16

Active members of the body, building the kingdom to a high standard.

God-Grounded · 2 Tim 3:16–17

A sober appreciation for Scripture — pursued regularly, shared freely.

Kingdom-Minded · 1 Thess 1:5–6

Ambassadors of God — living examples of His kingdom.

Embrace Vision & Passion · Hab 2:1–3

Looking forward with great expectation. God's passion flows out through us.
