# Sozo Loveland Venue — Qualification Stage

Purpose: filter every inquiry BEFORE any date is discussed. A date is never
checked, promised, or held until the lead clears this stage.

## Fit criteria (all must be true)

1. **Event type in scope** — corporate offsite/training, faith or community
   gathering, private event (wedding, shower, memorial, graduation), or
   music/arts (concert, worship night, rehearsal, recital).
2. **Headcount fits the room** — sanctuary flexible seating; confirm count
   against capacity before proceeding (capacity TBD with Steve Sr.).
3. **Date is plausibly open** — not a Sunday 10am–12pm, not a known church
   blackout (holidays, VBS, Easter/Christmas season, church conferences).
   Exact check happens in the approval stage; here we just rule out obvious
   conflicts.
4. **Budget meets the floor** — pricing tiers TBD (needs Steve + Steve Sr.
   tap). Until then: ask budget range on intake; anything under the eventual
   half-day floor is a no or a referral.
5. **Values alignment** — the event can't conflict with the church's mission.
   When in doubt, it goes to Steve Sr., not around him.

## Disqualifiers (instant no, polite decline)

- Event conflicts with church mission/values.
- Headcount exceeds safe capacity.
- Wants the date held with no deposit or signed agreement.
- Unwilling to carry event insurance if required for their event type.
- Timeline is under 7 days out and needs full production (judgment call —
   small simple gatherings can flex).

## Intake (collect before any date talk)

- Name + org + phone + email
- Event type + expected headcount
- Preferred date(s) + setup/teardown needs
- What they need: sound + engineer / lighting / livestream / rehearsal time
- Budget range
- How they heard about us (tracks which lane works)

## Outcomes

- **Qualified** → moves to the approval stage. Owner: Lauren.
- **Unqualified** → polite decline within 24h, offer referral if we have one.
- **Maybe** → parked with a reason + a revisit date. Nothing parked silently.

## SLA

Every inquiry gets a first response within 24 hours, even if it's just
"checking the calendar, back to you by [day]."
