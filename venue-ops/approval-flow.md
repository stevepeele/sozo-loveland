# Sozo Loveland Venue — Approval Stage

Runs only for qualified leads. Nothing here is promised until the deposit clears.

## Steps

1. **Calendar check** (Lauren, same day)
   Check the church calendar + blackout list against the requested date(s).
   If conflict: offer the two nearest open alternatives immediately.

2. **Pastor sign-off** (Steve Sr., within 48h)
   Every outside event gets his explicit yes — type, org, and date. No
   exceptions while this is new. Send him: who, what, headcount, date,
   setup needs, any values question.

3. **Quote** (Lauren, within 24h of sign-off)
   Send the priced quote: rental tier + add-ons (lighting, livestream,
   rehearsal time, hospitality) + cleaning fee + deposit terms. Quote
   valid 7 days.

4. **Contract + deposit** (renter)
   Simple rental agreement signed + deposit paid. THIS is the moment the
   date is held. Before this, the date is only "being checked."

5. **Confirmed on calendar** (Lauren)
   Date locked on the church calendar, renter gets a confirmation with
   load-in/load-out times, parking notes, and Steve's production contact.

6. **Pre-event check-in** (Steve, 7 days out)
   Walk the run of show: timeline, sound/lighting needs, vendor arrivals.
   No surprises on event day.

## Hard rules

- No date is promised, held, or announced until the deposit clears.
- Steve Sr. can veto any event, no justification required — it's his church.
- Pricing tiers + blackout calendar are still TBD — those are his + Steve's
  taps and they block step 3, not steps 1–2.
- Every approval, decline, and deposit is logged in the lead tracker so the
  pipeline is visible without asking.
