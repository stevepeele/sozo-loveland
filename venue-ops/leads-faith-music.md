## Notes & gaps
- 21 unique leads total (12 faith/community, 9 music/arts after deduping Greater Cincinnati Prayer Canopy, which appeared in both lanes).
- None of these leads have been contacted — research only, no outreach made.
- Recovery-ministry lane came up thin locally (the one with real details is in New Philadelphia, OH, ~3 hrs away) — dropped it; flag if the distance constraint loosens.
- Individual indie artists' album-release needs didn't surface direct contacts; that pitch works better through artist-community outlets like cincymusic.com than one-to-one.
- CCM (UC's College-Conservatory of Music) has its own halls and is a weak fit; private music schools are the stronger recital-space target.
- 4 leads have real profiles but no direct contact found (Christ Church Cincinnati, Grace Church Cincinnati, Come to the Table Cincinnati, Calvary Loveland Men's Ministry) — worth a targeted second pass.
