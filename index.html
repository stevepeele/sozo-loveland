Our Vision

The Journey in Three Steps

We know we're all on a faith journey. Being a Christian isn't easy, and it isn't finished in a day. When we accept Jesus, the journey begins — and none of us walk it perfectly, but in Christ we are being perfected every day.

We open our hearts to embrace one another and link together for the kingdom.
01

Open

We open our hearts — to God, to His Word, and to the people He's placed around us. Openness is where transformation starts.

02

Embrace

We embrace one another — welcoming every person as they are, the way Jesus would, and walking with them toward who God made them to be.

03

Link

We link together for the kingdom — joined arm in arm, generation to generation, building something bigger than any one of us.

What We Believe

Our Values

These aren't slogans on a wall. They're the convictions that shape how we worship, serve, and love.

Spirit Led

The Holy Spirit is in the earth today to lead us into the wisdom of God and guide every step of our lives.

Bible Based

The Bible is the inerrant Word of God — the foundation of what we believe and what we teach.

Family Friendly

The family is the foundation not only of the kingdom, but of Christians growing to maturity.

Preference Free

God is not a respecter of persons. He doesn't favor one group over another — and neither do we.

Independent

Christ is the head of the Church, and each church is given a unique role and purpose in His overall plan.

Team Based, Body Oriented

Each member is a vital part of the local church's ability to achieve its mission.

Generational

Each generation has something to give to the next — and something to receive from the last.

Authentic Faith · Luke 2:52

Our walk with God is both our experience and our reality. We use our faith to establish our unique voice and encourage others in theirs.

Open Hearts & Minds · 1 Cor 6:11

We relate to others like Jesus would — welcoming and accepting them as they are, letting God mold them into what He wants.

Relationships First · 2 Cor 5:16

Our goal is to know and to be known, as Scripture instructs — growing in relationship with God and with people.

Responsible & Accountable · Eph 4:14–16

We're active members of the body, accepting our call to build the kingdom. We hold ourselves to a high standard.

God-Grounded · 2 Tim 3:16–17

A sober appreciation for knowledge and Scripture — pursued regularly, shared with one another, growing always.

Kingdom-Minded · 1 Thess 1:5–6

We serve as ambassadors of God — living examples of what His kingdom is about and how others can grow in it.

Embrace Vision & Passion · Hab 2:1–3

Always looking forward with great expectation and positive words — unwilling to simply go with the flow. God's passion fills us, and we let it flow out.
