Why SOZO?

SOZO is the Greek word the New Testament uses for the full work of Jesus — not just forgiveness, but wholeness. Every time you read "saved" in Scripture, there's a good chance the word underneath is sozo. It's our name because it's what we're about.

SAVED

Rescued

Brought out of darkness and into life. Grace that meets you exactly where you are.

HEALED

Restored

Made whole — body, mind, and spirit. God still heals, still restores, still makes broken things new.

DELIVERED

Set Free

Released from what held you. Freedom you can actually walk in, every day.
