Plan Your Visit

Come to SOZO.

Welcome to an experience with God that promises to meet every need, answer every question, and fix everything that is broken. That is the SOZO life — and we want to walk it with you.

What’s Important to Us

Teaching the Word of God is the heart of this ministry.

That’s reflected in our practical Sunday messages and classes. We believe God wants us to go deeper in scripture — to be nourished on meat, not just milk.

We value worship through anointed music and praise — not only on Sundays, but as a priority in every area of our daily lives.

We seek the presence and ministry of the Holy Spirit, and we encourage the exercise and development of spiritual gifts in the lives of believers.

And family marks this ministry in a distinguishing way. As born-again believers we are all part of the same family — and God created us in His image regardless of nationality, race, language, tribe, or nation. The ethnic diversity of our congregation is a testimony to that truth.

Common Questions

What should I expect?

What time should I come?

Bible study starts at 9:00 AM; the main service runs 10AM–12PM. SOZO Kids check-in opens at 10:15 AM — come a few minutes early your first time.

Where do I park?

Right at the building — 9781 Fields Ertel Rd, Loveland. Plenty of parking, and our hospitality team will guide you from the lot to the door.

What do I wear?

Whatever you’re comfortable in. You’ll see everything from jeans to Sunday best.

What about my kids?

SOZO Kids is a vibrant, Bible-based ministry from nursery (birth–age 2) through Jungle House (1st–6th grade). Check-in starts at 10:15 AM.

How long is the service?

About two hours — worship, practical teaching, and time to connect.

Do I have to be a Christian to come?

Not at all. You don’t have to be perfect to be here — just willing to learn and grow.
