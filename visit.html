    Common Questions

    What should I expect?

    What time should I come?

    Bible study starts at 9:00 AM; the main service runs 10AM–12PM. SOZO Kids check-in opens at 10:15 AM — come a few minutes early your first time.

    Where do I park?

    Right at the building — 9781 Fields Ertel Rd, Loveland. Plenty of parking, and our hospitality team will guide you from the lot to the door.

    What do I wear?

    Whatever you’re comfortable in. You’ll see everything from jeans to Sunday best.

    What about my kids?

    SOZO Kids is a vibrant, Bible-based ministry from nursery (birth–age 2) through 6th grade. Check-in starts at 10:15 AM.

    How long is the service?

    About two hours — worship, practical teaching, and time to connect.

    Do I have to be a Christian to come?

    Not at all. You don’t have to be perfect to be here — just willing to learn and grow.

    SOZO is a Greek word that captures the gospel in a single word — salvation, healing, and redemption. In the big picture, it means “wholeness.”
