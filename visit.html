Good to Know

What to Expect

What should I wear?

Whatever you're comfortable in — jeans are fine. Nobody's keeping score.

What about my kids?

SOZO Kids (nursery–5th grade) runs during the service. Our team will help you check in — it takes two minutes.

How long is the service?

About 75 minutes — worship, a practical Bible-based message, and time to connect after.

Do I have to give?

No. Giving is for our church family. As a guest, your presence is the gift — seriously.

Where do I park?

Right out front at 9781 Fields Ertel Rd. Look for the greeters in the lobby — they'll point you wherever you need to go.
